Delete your data

Pocket Puzzles (com.konark.puzzlelab), by Konark Jha

Pocket Puzzles has no account of its own and the developer keeps no copy of your data. What exists, and how to delete it:

1. Levels and settings on your phone

Open the game, then Settings > Reset progress, or uninstall the app. Both delete them straight away.

2. Saved progress in Google Play Games (only if you signed in)

Your levels are kept in your own Play Games account. To delete them: open the Google Play Games app (or play.google.com/games/profile) > Settings > Delete Play Games account and data, and choose Pocket Puzzles (or delete your whole Play Games profile). Google deletes the saved game, your player identity for this game and the related diagnostics, as described in Google's privacy policy. Nothing is kept by the developer.

3. Ask us

Write to hello@locomojo.in with the subject "Delete my data" and we will help you within 30 days.
