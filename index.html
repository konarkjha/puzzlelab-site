Puzzle Lab

Three puzzle games in one Android app (com.konark.puzzlelab).

Snake Out

Tap a snake and it slides off the board the way its head points. Free them all in an order that works.

Colour Pour

Pour coloured liquid between tubes until every tube holds one colour.

Cheese Run

Lead the mouse across the lawn, eat every piece of cheese in one run and stay clear of the cats.
