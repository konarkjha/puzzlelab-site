Privacy Policy

Pocket Puzzles (com.konark.puzzlelab). Last updated: 19 November 2026 (Play Games section added)

Short version: the game has no account of its own and no server of its own. Google Play Games, which the game uses, collects some diagnostics and usage data, and saves your levels in your own Play Games account when you sign in.

1. What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer of Pocket Puzzles runs no server for this game and the game sends no information to the developer.

2. What is stored on your phone

The level you have reached in each game and your settings (sound, vibration, snake skin). Removing the app removes them.

3. Google Play Games

The game uses Google Play Games Services. Its software, provided by Google, collects some data automatically to keep it working and to improve it: diagnostics and usage (analytics) data about the Play Games features. If you sign in with Play Games, Google also receives your Play Games player identity (gamer name and avatar), and the level you have reached in each game is saved in your own Play Games account (Saved Games) so that it comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone. This data is sent over an encrypted connection and handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer can read only your own saved progress for this game. You can delete it, or your whole Play Games profile, at any time from the Play Games app or your Google account.

4. Ads

Test versions of the game show no ads. A public version may offer optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob; when it does, Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar device information to choose and measure ads, as described in how Google uses information for ads. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. This policy will be updated when ads are switched on.

5. Purchases

If the game offers a purchase, it is handled by Google Play. The developer never sees your card or bank details.

6. What the game does not do

It does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, microphone or location, and it has no chat.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
